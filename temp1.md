# Lessons Learned

* **Plan module dependencies upfront:** Identify cross-module dependencies during planning and, where possible, assign dependent modules to the same developer. This reduces coordination effort, avoids waiting for dependent version releases, and enables faster end-to-end implementation.

* **Validate all required access before starting:** Perform a comprehensive access review before development begins to ensure all necessary permissions are available, preventing avoidable delays during implementation.

* **Clarify requirements end-to-end:** Review and align on requirements, edge cases, dependencies, and the implementation approach before development starts to minimize rework and reduce the need for follow-up clarifications.

* **Overall:** Strong team collaboration and support helped us successfully navigate challenges and deliver the implementation. The experience provided valuable learnings that will improve our planning, execution, and collaboration in future projects.
